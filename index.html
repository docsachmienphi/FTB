Why From The Beginning?

Every big company was once a small idea in a kitchen, a garage, or a backyard. A sick pharmacist stirring syrup. Two brothers making shoes. A man selling books from his garage.

On this website you can read a short version of each story - companies, people, and inventions - learn the most surprising facts, and then watch the full story on YouTube.

Popular Topics

  • Company History: Coca-Cola, Nike, Adidas, Amazon, Toyota, Walmart, Tesla, Nvidia, Apple, Starbucks, and many more
  • Founder Stories: Elon Musk, Jensen Huang, Steve Jobs - the people behind the brands, and how they began
  • Great Inventions: how famous things were invented, and the hands that made them real
