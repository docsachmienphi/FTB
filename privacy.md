# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 12, 2026*

From The Beginning is a small storytelling website. We tell the stories of how companies, founders, and inventions began, in slow and simple English. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you visit.

## The short version

We do not ask you to sign up. We do not have accounts, comments, or a contact form. We do not run advertising, and we do not use analytics tools to follow you around. Reading a story here is like reading a quiet book in a library — you simply read, and that is all.

## What we collect

**Nothing directly.** This website has no forms, no logins, and no comment boxes, so we never receive your name, your email address, or anything you type.

Like almost every website, our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (such as your IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the site online. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; you can read [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for how they handle those logs.

## Cookies

This website does not set any cookies of its own.

Two features can involve other companies' services:

- **Videos.** Each story page shows a still picture of its video. The video player itself loads only when you press play, and it uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com). When you play a video, YouTube and Google may use cookies and collect information under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Fonts.** Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser talks to Google's servers to fetch them.

## Share buttons

The share buttons at the end of each story are simple links. "Share on Facebook" and "Share on X" take you to those services' own share windows, where their privacy policies apply. "Copy Link" only copies the page address inside your own browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

## Links to other websites

Our stories link to sources and further reading on other websites, and to our YouTube channel. Once you follow a link and leave this site, that website's own privacy policy applies. We choose our links carefully, but we are not responsible for the practices of other sites.

## Children

This website is written in simple English and is safe for general audiences, including learners of all ages. Because we collect no personal information at all, we collect none from children either.

## Changes to this policy

If our practices ever change — for example, if we add a contact form, analytics, or advertising — we will update this page first and note the date at the top. Any future service of that kind will be described here before it is used.

## How to reach us

For now, the best way to reach From The Beginning is through our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@FromTheBeginning9). We read our viewers' comments with warm hearts.

Thank you for reading, and for being here at the beginning.
